The story so far

Trained in adtech.
Built for the big leagues.

I spent my career where audiences are loudest — adtech platforms and the livestreaming industry — leading marketing for data companies that count viewers by the millions. As Marketing Director at LIVAD and a four-year veteran of Stream Hatchet, I learned to translate raw audience data into stories brands act on.

Now I'm aiming that experience at sports. Not as a career change — as a homecoming. I grew up on rinks, pitches, and arenas. I write about sports marketing weekly, I volunteer at championships, and I believe women's sports is the biggest audience opportunity of the next decade.

If your team needs someone who reads a media plan like a box score — this site is for you.
